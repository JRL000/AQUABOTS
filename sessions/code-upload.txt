  Push the camera fix
  From a bash shell in the repo root (C:\Users\johnr\AQUABOTS_GitHub):
  bash ./push-to-jetson.sh webrtc
  That single command:
  - scp's jetson/webrtc_sender.py to /home/mayra/webrtc_jetson.py on the Jetson
  - restarts the webrtc service
  - prints the last 30 log lines

  What "success" looks like in the log
  - [CAM] GStreamer pipeline started
  - No Failed to create CaptureSession
  - No NvBufSurfaceFromFd errors

  Then open http://100.76.1.26:5000 and confirm the video panel is live.

  If it fails
  - Failed to create CaptureSession → CAM1 isn't seeing the sensor. Check ribbon cable on both ends (contacts toward the board, latches closed), then full power-cycle the Jetson (unplug power, wait 10 s, plug back in — not just a reboot, kernel needs to re-probe IMX219).
  - sudo dmesg | grep imx219 on the Jetson should print a bound line — if not, the sensor isn't being detected at the driver level.
  - Re-run bash ./push-to-jetson.sh webrtc after the power-cycle.
